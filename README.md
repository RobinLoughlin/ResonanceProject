Resonance simulation

Overview: I am creating a simulation of a damped simple harmonic oscillator that then plots a graph of its resonance peak

Requirements: Python3, NumPy, Matplotlib

How to run: python Resonance.py in terminal

Simulation features:
-Plots displacement of a damped harmonic oscillator in time whilst under the influence of a driving frequency
-Plots the maximum displacement of the oscilaltor when driving frequency is varied to graphically show resonance. It does this by plotting the natural frequency of the oscillator (sqrt(k/m)) as a red dashed line, and it plots the driving frequency of the oscillator as a green dotted line. Resonance occurs when the red and green lines overlap

Purpose of the project:
The aim of this project was to graphically verify resonance of an oscillator, whilst developing my numerical simulation skills
